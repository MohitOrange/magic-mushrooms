Our Story

From our farm house to your family's plate

Magic Mushrooms is a local mushroom farm house in Dindigul. We grow Oyster and Milky mushrooms in clean, controlled rooms — no chemicals, no shortcuts, just patient natural farming.

Every box is harvested fresh, packed hygienically and delivered the same day — so homes, restaurants and hotels get the best taste and nutrition.
